The Restaurant

Where Lisbon gathers after dark

Interior photo

ococas is a candle-lit dining room in the heart of Príncipe Real, named for the little frog that has watched over the house since 2019.

Set in a restored townhouse off the garden, we kept the bones of old Lisbon — high ceilings, dark timber, brass — and filled them with the energy of a modern dining room. Evenings move from quiet dinners to a humming bar.

Our kitchen reads the Portuguese canon closely, then turns it: clams and coriander, suckling pig and orange, scarlet prawns and bread. Familiar, then surprising.

“We cook the Portugal we grew up with — only a little braver.”
— The kitchen at ococas
